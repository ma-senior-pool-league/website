---
title: Tie-breakers (TB's)
order: 3
---
Format to be used when matches are tied after regulation:

1. A 2 games-out-of-3 series is played after each team, without knowing opponent's line-up, makes
   out a TB three-man batting order;
2. Visitors choose table for TB1 with breaker decided by coin flip called by visitors;
3. At the same time on the other table TB2, with the other team breaking, the second players in the
   batting order square off;
4. If a rubber game is necessary, it will be played by the third players in the batting orders on
   the same table as TB1 with the same team breaking as broke in TB1.
