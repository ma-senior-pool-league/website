*For the Senior Travel 8-ball league and K.I.S.S. Tourneys.*

**A.** When racking, the 8-ball goes in the middle, any ball goes in front; and, in the other
corners, you put one high ball and one low ball.

**B.** For each of the games played in a match appoint a referee, who should sit or stand quietly
and help out when needed. If players fail to do so, he should call fouls or stop a player from
shooting at the wrong ball.

**C.** No distracting noise or movements, cursing, or arguing loudly.

**D.** No kibitzing or coaching (with the exception that each team is permitted four time-outs,
which can be used at any time during the match). Any conferring with a player, *even between
shots*, constitutes coaching.

1. A player requesting a time-out may only receive advise from one member of his own team, not
   multiple members at the same time. A different team member may give advice for each separate
   time out.
2. During a requested time out only verbal advice is allowed. No demonstrations, no touching the
   balls, cue stick, bridges, etc. No marking the table cushions to assist with aiming. Pointing is
   OK.

**E.** Don't pick up the rack, dismantle or put away your stick, or remove balls from a pocket
before your opponent has completed his last shot. (Penalty: loss of game)

**F.** In the Travel League matches, the starting four players (if they are subbed for) may
re-enter, but they must stay in their proper spot in the batting order (that is, next to the number
or letter they started with).

**G.** Substitutes in the Travel League may play a game for anybody at any time.

**H.** Except for tie-breakers (TB's), no one (substitute or starter) may play the same opponent
more than once, or play more than four games in a regulation 16-game match.

**I.** At the end of the regular season the division winners compete in a 2 match-out-of-3 playoff
series. The team with the better over-all, regular-season record has the home advantage. If the
division champs have the same record, flip a coin for home advantage.

**J.** Under-age players forfeit all their games.

**K.** ***No postponements:*** Once the schedule has been printed up and accepted, there will be no
changes except in emergencies (e.g. hurricanes, terrorist attacks, etc., and even then, only If
okayed by the commissioner's office). Every team should have stand-by players. If they are not
available, contact Dick Carey, who can usually find a replacement player or two. If not, you will
have to play shorthanded.

*Note: In July 2024, there were 2 additions made to the time-out rules in item D, above. The
